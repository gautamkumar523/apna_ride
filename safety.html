Safety centre

Every ride, watched over from pickup to drop.

Safety isn't a settings menu nobody opens. It's built into every trip by default — here's exactly what protects you on the way there.

🆔

Verified captains

Every captain completes ID verification and a background check before accepting their first ride, and re-verifies periodically.

📍

Live trip sharing

Share your live route, captain details and ETA with anyone in one tap — they can follow along without installing anything.

🚨

24/7 SOS button

Visible on every trip screen. One press alerts our safety response team and can connect you to local emergency services.

🎙️

Audio trip recording

Optional in-trip audio recording that's encrypted and only accessed to investigate a reported safety concern.

⭐

Two-way ratings

Riders and captains rate each trip. Consistently low ratings trigger a review, and repeat issues lead to removal from the platform.

🧭

Route monitoring

Significant, unexplained detours or long unplanned stops are flagged automatically for our safety team to check in.

In an emergency

What happens when you press SOS

  • ✓ Your live location and trip details are sent instantly to our safety response team
  • ✓ Your emergency contacts are notified with your location, if you've added any
  • ✓ A safety team member calls you within moments to confirm you're okay
  • ✓ If needed, local emergency services are alerted with your exact location
