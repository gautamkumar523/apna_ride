Our story

Built in India, for the way India actually commutes.

Apna Ride started with a simple observation: most people don't need a fancy ride, they need an affordable one that shows up on time. Everything since has been built around that.

Live in 4 districts, built for all of India

Apna Ride began with a simple idea: bring affordable, upfront-priced rides to commuters priced out of cabs and tired of unpredictable auto fares. Riders wanted to know the price before they got in — so that became the rule for every ride on the platform, not a feature.

Today, Apna Ride is live across Patna, Aurangabad, Motihari (East Champaran) and Bettiah (West Champaran) districts — bike taxis, autos, cabs and parcel delivery, reaching towns and villages most ride apps don't. We're building out from here, with the rest of India next.

What we believe

Three ideas that shape every decision

💸

Affordable by design

Fares are set to reflect what people actually earn in the cities we operate in — not what the market will bear.

🤝

Fair to captains

A captain's time and fuel cost money. Our commission structure is built to keep driving worthwhile.

🏙️

Every city, not just the big ones

Tier 2 and tier 3 cities get the same app, the same safety tools, and the same fare transparency as any metro.
